Issue

The broader social justice issue we are focusing on and trying to solve is fast fashion and the idea of the wasting of goods. Fast fashion is the business model described as rapidly, designing and producing, and selling inexpensive garments in high volumes. As a sub issue, we are looking into the issue of child labour and enslavement. Fast fashion relies on ultra-low prices and rapid production cycles, which pushes factories and suppliers to cut cost and hire child labour, as children are often paid very little.

Our plan

Our plan to solve the issue is to create an enterprise that takes in old clothing donations from the community and takes them apart and remakes them into new clothes. Our idea supports our want to solve the issue of child labour and fast fashion by; reducing demand for new clothes, promoting ethical clothing/fashion, raising awareness, and creating an alternative to manufactured clothing that most likely would have been produced from enslaved children.

Charity

Our charity is Goodweave and Lifeline. We are supporting Goodweave as they similarly share a passion for ending child labour and child enslavement. We are also supporting lifeline as we can collect clothing donations from their stores to create our reused clothing pieces.

1 shirt represents 40 pieces of clothing that are discarded or burned every second.
